Version2.6.0

Released Nov 26, 2026 · 9 new features

How to update

Install 2.6.0 right over the app you have. Your tasks, check marks, goals, and settings all stay.Still on the original Morning Missions 1.0? Export a backup first, then follow section 3 of the user guide.

New in this patch

9 features

More ways to repeat

Every 2, 3, or 4 weeks, monthly on a date or a weekday (like the third Friday), or every few days.

FIND ITTask editor > Repeats

Overnight tasks

Plan tasks that run past midnight, like a 10 PM to 6 AM shift.

FIND ITSet the end time before the start

Task notes

Add details to any task. The first line shows right under it.

FIND ITTask editor > Notes

Task history

See how often a task got done, your streaks, and a calendar of every day.

FIND ITTask editor > History, or Month screen

Undo and redo

Step back through your last 30 changes, and put them back.

FIND ITUndo arrow, top right

Home-screen widget

Today’s tasks and your progress, right on your home screen.

FIND ITHold your home screen > Widgets

Goal reminders

A heads-up before a goal’s date, on the day itself, and a weekly check-in.

FIND ITTools > Notifications

Any task counts

One-day tasks can now count toward your goals too.

FIND ITTask editor > Counts toward a goal

Southern seasons

Season names for goal dates can follow the Southern Hemisphere.

FIND ITTools > Look > Seasons
