    Repeating tasks

    Past days always keep the tasks they had. Dragging to reorder applies to every day that shares those tasks.

    Storage on this device

    A day's score is just "done / total", a few bytes. Archived days still show in Month view, without their task list.

    Backups

    A backup holds your routine, check marks, notes, and settings. Keep one somewhere safe before you switch phones or reinstall the app.

    Restore also reads backups from the older Morning Missions tracker. The HTML copy is the whole planner with your data inside it.

    Weekly records

    A readable snapshot of one week, laid out like a printed schedule.

    Records for older weeks are in Month view.

    Start over

    Erase every task, check mark, and note on this device and go back to the starter routine.
